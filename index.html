      HOW TO HOP

      Step into any empty neighboring hole.

      Hop over any neighboring marble—any color—into the empty hole beyond. Keep hopping as far as the glowing dots allow.

      Win by moving all 10 of your marbles into the point directly opposite your start. The first finisher ends the game.
